## Attribution handling

Each campaign folder contains the exact credit in `ATTRIBUTION.txt`.
`clipfactory.create scenery` automatically appends that line to the generated
TikTok caption metadata. Do not remove it unless you obtain a separate paid
license from Incompetech.

No warranty is provided. Other platform terms may apply; preserve the credit
where a reasonable viewer can find it (post caption/description or profile
music credits).
